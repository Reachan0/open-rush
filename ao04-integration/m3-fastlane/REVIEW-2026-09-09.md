## Findings not adopted as stated

- **Cancel must kill the dependency immediately:** the agreed contract is
  cooperative cancellation of operations, with bounded HTTP I/O. An already
  in-flight call may finish; its result cannot become `ok` after cancellation,
  queued calls cannot enter, and no retries follow. Destroying a healthy shared
  dependency is not required by the M3 acceptance boundary. The stronger
  cancellation scenario now checks both controller responses and the dependency
  call count.
- **Shared timeout budget must be a wall-clock deadline:** the approved M3
  budget is three total timeout attempts shared through instance serialization
  and circuit locking. Each actual local HTTP request has a finite timeout.
  Arbitrary unbounded third-party dependency implementations are outside this
  adapter's present contract.
- **Reconfirm baseline whenever its timestamp/generation changes:** baseline is
  admission evidence. Once admitted, a failed dependency must reach incident
  handling, not be reclassified as setup failure. This preserves the earlier
  review's explicit requirement.

## Evidence limits

Hashes detect changes and the verifier rejects specific inconsistent evidence.
They do not authenticate a malicious actor who rewrites the runner, verifier and
all evidence together. Runtime acceptance does not prove the full OpenRush web
Run lifecycle or statistical reliability. Original research batches remain
separate.
